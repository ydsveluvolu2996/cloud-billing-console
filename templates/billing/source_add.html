Which account scope?

  • Single AWS account (including member): connects only the account you enter. You do not need access to its organization's management account.
  • Management / payer: connects an organization through its management account. Choose this only when you intend to collect linked accounts. It uses the reviewed approval workflow.
  • Member budget reader: imports budgets created in an account whose costs are already collected through a payer.

For single-account and member-budget connections, the customer administrator creates one read-only role. An internal portfolio administrator then chooses Connect and import in the dashboard; no hosting-account or EC2 sign-in is needed for each supported connection.

Accounts already covered by a connected payer cannot be connected twice. Shared payer is only for an organization serving multiple customers.
