Which kind?

  • Management / payer: one read-only role in the payer account collects every linked account’s costs. Member accounts never need a role.
  • Standalone: an account outside any organization you collect.
  • Member budget reader: optional. Reads AWS Budgets created inside a member account without collecting its costs again.

Duplicate detection rejects accounts already connected elsewhere or already covered by a connected payer, so no spend is collected twice.

Shared payer: tick when several customers share one organization. Each linked account then needs an explicit assignment; unassigned accounts enter a review queue.
