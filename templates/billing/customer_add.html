How onboarding works

  1. Create the customer and, optionally, a monthly customer budget.
  2. Add each management/payer or standalone account as its own connection.
  3. Share the per-connection CloudFormation link; the customer creates one read-only role.
  4. The worker verifies the role, discovers linked accounts and queues the initial import.
  5. Assign accounts and projects, then configure budgets.

One payer connection covers its whole organization. Member accounts never need their own role. Nothing is emailed automatically; you share the link yourself.
