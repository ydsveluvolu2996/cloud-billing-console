How onboarding works

  1. Create the customer and, optionally, a monthly customer budget.
  2. Add a Single AWS account (including member) connection for the account you want to onboard.
  3. The customer administrator creates one read-only IAM role. Save its ARN, then an internal portfolio administrator with current MFA confirms authorization and chooses Connect account.
  4. When AWS access and account discovery pass, the dashboard starts the first data pull automatically. Collection continues every six hours.

Single-account setup connects only the account you enter, even when it belongs to another organization. You do not need its payer account.

Management and shared payer connections use the reviewed approval workflow for their authorized account inventory. A payer role can collect member costs; budgets created inside a member account may need a separate member budget reader.

No customer CloudFormation stack, access keys or passwords are required. Nothing is emailed automatically.
